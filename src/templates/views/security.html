GNOME Keyring & Cold-Boot Autounlock

Keyring Setup Guide

Resolve the post-boot "Unlock Login Keyring" prompt so you can log in 100% passwordlessly with just your fingerprint.

Why does Linux prompt for your password after a cold boot or shutdown?

Linux encrypts your keyring with your password. When you log in with your fingerprint after restarting or shutting down, the sensor verifies your identity but cannot provide your text password to decrypt stored secrets (Wi-Fi passwords, browser login tokens). Unlocking from screen lock does not prompt because your session was already decrypted in memory.

How to Enable 100% Passwordless Boot

  1. Click Open Passwords & Keys (Seahorse) below.
  2. In the left sidebar under Passwords, right-click Login (or Default Keyring) and select Change Password.
  3. Enter your current login password when prompted.
  4. Leave both New Password and Confirm Password completely blank and click Continue.
  5. Confirm the prompt "Store passwords unencrypted".
